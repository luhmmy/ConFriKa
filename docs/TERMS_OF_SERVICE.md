## 2. Description of Service

ConFriKa is a pan-African language learning, real-time dialect translation, direct messaging, and voice calling platform designed to unify African communication across 54 sovereign nations.

Key features include:
- **Tone Coach & Dialect Learning:** Real-time acoustic pitch comparison and tonal disambiguation.
- **Dynamic Translation:** Multi-dialect translation with diacritic preservation across respectful, formal, and casual registers.
- **Direct Messaging & Global Spaces:** Instant messaging with live translation toggles.
- **In-App Voice Calling:** Low-latency WebRTC voice communication.
- **No-App Mobile Calling (Coming Soon):** Upcoming feature to bridge cellular PSTN telephony, currently under active carrier interconnect review.
