Privacy Policy

ConFriKa · confrika.online · Last updated 24 October 2026

ConFriKa (“we”, “us”) helps people across Africa talk to each other across languages. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account: your name, @username, email, country, origin region and chosen dialect, and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Content you create: messages, community posts, images, and voice recordings you choose to make (tone practice, voice enrollment).
  • Device: a push-notification token so we can alert you to messages and calls.
  • Usage: basic activity needed to run features (for example your learning streak).

Encryption of direct messages

Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted on your device. We store only the ciphertext and cannot read your private messages. Your private key never leaves your device. Public community posts are not end-to-end encrypted.

Translation and voice

To translate text and speech we process the content needed to perform the translation. In-call speech translation and voice enrollment send the relevant audio to our translation and voice provider to produce the translation and, if you enroll, a voice model. We do not sell your content.

How we use information

  • To provide messaging, calls, translation, communities and tonal coaching.
  • To send you OTP codes, notifications and account emails.
  • To keep the service safe (rate limiting, abuse and ban enforcement).

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that run core features on our behalf (email delivery, push notifications, translation and voice, hosting), and when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices and rights

  • Reachability: choose who can call or message you, and whether people can send you images.
  • Access & correction: edit your profile in the app.
  • Deletion: delete your account at any time from Profile → “Delete my account”. This permanently removes your account, messages and contacts.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account data.

Children

ConFriKa is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Contact

Questions: privacy@confrika.online
